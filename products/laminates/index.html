What to check before ordering

Thickness

Enquire for 0.8 mm, 0.92 mm, 1 mm or 1.5 mm options. Availability depends on the selected brand and collection.

Design code

Send the catalogue name and exact design number, including any finish suffix such as HG.

Surface finish

Compare woodgrain, solid colours, gloss and texture options. A physical sample is useful because screens can display colours differently.

Quantity

State the number of sheets, sheet size and whether matching designs or edge finishes are required.

Plan your enquiry

A complete enquiry might read: “Woodline laminate, 1 mm, design 1646HG, 10 sheets, delivery Gorakhpur.” Confirm current stock and price before placing the order.
