What to check before ordering

Application

Mention shelves, shutters, wall panels, routed designs or other furniture components so the board can be assessed for that use.

Board grade

Confirm whether you require standard or moisture-resistant MDF. Suitability depends on the manufacturer’s specification; do not assume every MDF board is waterproof.

Surface

State whether you need plain board for finishing or a prefinished surface, and describe the planned paint or laminate.

Size and quantity

Share thickness in mm, sheet dimensions and number of sheets, along with any preferred brand.

Plan your enquiry

Ask your carpenter to confirm the required thickness, fixing method and edge treatment. Request the product specification when comparing MDF with plywood or HDHMR.
