What to check before ordering

Moisture conditions

Describe the intended location and exposure to moisture. Moisture resistance does not automatically mean a board is suitable for continuous water exposure.

Specifications

Check the manufacturer, product grade and technical sheet when comparing boards. Avoid relying only on appearance or a generic category name.

Finishing

Discuss the planned surface finish, edge sealing and hardware fixing with your carpenter or installer.

Quotation details

Send thickness, sheet size, number of sheets, brand preference and delivery location.
