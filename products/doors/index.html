What to check before ordering

Measurements

Provide the opening height and width, required door thickness and frame details. Confirm the final measurements with your installer.

Location

Mention whether the door is for an entrance, bedroom, bathroom or another area, including expected moisture exposure.

Finish

Share a design reference and whether you need a finished door or a separate decorative surface. Doorskin and a complete door are different items.

Accessories

List hinges, locks, handles and frame requirements separately so the scope of supply is clear.

Plan your enquiry

Send the number of doors with a measurement for each opening. Confirm whether the quotation includes the frame, hardware, delivery and installation.
